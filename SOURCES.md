## Why this is not claimed to be “every laptop company in India”

The Indian laptop market includes global OEMs, local brands, import-only sellers, short-lived brands, white-label devices, marketplace-only sellers, and companies whose public contact structure changes frequently. A finite static list cannot honestly guarantee literally every active seller. This project therefore starts with a meaningful, source-backed set and gives you a safe workflow for adding more companies as you verify them.
